1.Abhishek Sharma

About Abhishek Sharma

Abhishek Sharma is one of India’s most exciting young T20 cricketers. He first gained national attention as a talented all-rounder in the India Under-19 setup and quickly became known for his fearless batting.

In domestic cricket, he represents Punjab and developed a reputation as an aggressive top-order batter who attacks from the first ball. His ability to dominate the power-play overs made him a standout performer in the shortest format.

His big breakthrough came in the IPL with Sunrisers Hyderabad, where he became famous for explosive opening partnerships and consistent power-hitting. By 2026, he is considered one of the key young players shaping India’s future T20 squad.

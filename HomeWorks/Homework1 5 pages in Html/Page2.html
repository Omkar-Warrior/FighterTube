The First Victory – Torna Fort

At just 16 years old, Shivaji Maharaj dreamed of building Swarajya (self-rule) when India was ruled by powerful kingdoms. While others doubted his age, Shivaji believed in his vision.

Instead of fighting a huge war, he used strategy and intelligence to capture Torna Fort from the Adilshahi Sultanate. This was the very first fort he conquered.

This small victory became the foundation of the Maratha Empire and proved that great journeys begin with small steps.

Escape from Panhala Fort

Shivaji Maharaj was once trapped inside Panhala Fort by a huge enemy army. The situation looked impossible and defeat seemed near.

But Shivaji planned a brilliant midnight escape during heavy rain. His loyal warrior Baji Prabhu Deshpande stayed behind and fought thousands of enemies at Pavan Khind to protect his king.

This sacrifice saved Shivaji Maharaj and became a symbol of loyalty, bravery, and sacrifice.

The Afzal Khan Encounter

The powerful general Afzal Khan invited Shivaji Maharaj for a peace meeting but secretly planned to attack him.

Shivaji Maharaj sensed the danger and wore hidden armor and carried the Wagh Nakh (tiger claws) for protection.

When Afzal Khan attacked first, Shivaji defended himself and defeated him. This victory shocked powerful kingdoms and proved his courage and intelligence.

The Birth of Indian Navy

Shivaji Maharaj realized that enemies were attacking India from the sea routes and no king had focused on naval power before.

He built India’s first strong navy and constructed sea forts like Sindhudurg to protect the coastline.

This visionary step made him the Father of the Indian Navy.

Coronation of Chhatrapati

In 1674, Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort after years of struggle and victories.

This event marked the birth of Hindavi Swarajya — a kingdom ruled for the people and by the people.

His coronation became a symbol of freedom, pride, and self-rule in Indian history.

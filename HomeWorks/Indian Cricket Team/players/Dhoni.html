About

MS Dhoni, also known as “Captain Cool,” is one of the greatest cricketers in the history of Indian cricket. Born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, India, Dhoni became famous for his calm leadership, powerful batting, and excellent wicketkeeping skills. He led the Indian cricket team to many historic victories, including the 2007 ICC T20 World Cup, the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, and the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy. Dhoni is widely admired for his ability to finish matches under pressure and his iconic helicopter shot. His leadership style, confidence, and dedication have inspired millions of cricket fans and young players around the world. Even after stepping down from international cricket, he remains one of the most respected and influential figures in the sport.

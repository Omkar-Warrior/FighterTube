About

KL Rahul is one of the most stylish and technically sound batsmen in Indian cricket. Born on 18 April 1992 in Mangalore, Karnataka, Rahul is known for his elegant batting style and ability to play in multiple formats of the game. He is a versatile player who can open the batting or play in the middle order. Rahul is also an excellent wicketkeeper in limited-overs cricket. Over the years, he has played many important innings for India and has been praised for his calm approach and consistency. His dedication, discipline, and adaptability have made him a key player for the Indian cricket team.

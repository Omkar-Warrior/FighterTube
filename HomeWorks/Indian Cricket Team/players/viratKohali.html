About

Virat Kohli is one of the greatest modern-day cricketers and one of the most consistent batsmen in the history of cricket. Born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India, Kohli is famous for his aggressive batting style, strong determination, and excellent leadership skills. He has played many match-winning innings for the Indian cricket team and has broken numerous batting records in international cricket. Virat Kohli is widely admired for his fitness, discipline, and passion for the game. He has led the Indian team in all formats of cricket and inspired millions of young players around the world. Because of his consistency and hunger for runs, he is often called the **"King of Cricket."**

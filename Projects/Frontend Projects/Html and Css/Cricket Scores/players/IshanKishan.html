About

Ishan Kishan is a talented Indian cricketer known for his aggressive batting and excellent wicketkeeping skills. Born on 18 July 1998 in Patna, Bihar, Ishan Kishan quickly made a name for himself in domestic cricket with his fearless approach and powerful stroke play. He gained recognition after leading the Indian team in the Under-19 Cricket World Cup and later became a key player in the Indian Premier League. Ishan is known for his ability to score quick runs and play attacking cricket. His confidence, energy, and strong performances have made him one of the most promising young cricketers in Indian cricket.

About

Jasprit Bumrah is one of the best fast bowlers in modern cricket and a key player for the Indian cricket team. Born on 6 December 1993 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Bumrah is known for his unique bowling action, deadly yorkers, and ability to perform under pressure. He has become one of the most successful bowlers in all formats of the game. Bumrah played an important role in many victories for India with his pace, accuracy, and wicket-taking ability. He is especially famous for his performances in limited-overs cricket and in the death overs. His hard work, discipline, and determination have made him one of the most respected bowlers in international cricket.

About

Rohit Sharma is one of the most talented and elegant batsmen in modern cricket. Born on 30 April 1987 in Nagpur, India, he is famous for his calm batting style and ability to score big runs. Rohit is popularly known as the “Hitman” of Indian cricket because of his powerful stroke play and record-breaking performances. He is the only player in the world to score three double centuries in One Day International (ODI) cricket. Rohit Sharma has played many match-winning innings for India and has also led the Indian cricket team in international tournaments. His leadership and batting skills have inspired millions of cricket fans and young players around the world.

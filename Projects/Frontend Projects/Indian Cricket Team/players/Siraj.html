About

Mohammed Siraj is one of the most promising fast bowlers in Indian cricket. Born on 13 March 1994 in Hyderabad, India, Siraj is known for his aggressive bowling style, speed, and ability to take wickets in crucial moments. Coming from a humble background, Siraj worked extremely hard to reach the international level and became an inspiration for many young cricketers. He gained recognition through his performances in domestic cricket and the Indian Premier League. Siraj has played an important role in several Indian victories with his pace, swing bowling, and determination on the field.
